Our Evolution

From Visionary Studio to Premier Turnkey Builder

Established in 2008 in Bengaluru, SAN Construction began with a simple yet ambitious mandate: to elevate Indian residential architecture by marrying climate-responsive traditional layouts with precision modern engineering.

Over two decades, we have expanded operations across Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad. Today, our multidisciplinary team of 120+ architects, structural engineers, and project managers handles every milestone from soil testing and municipal approvals to final interior handcrafted woodwork.

Our Vision

To be South India’s most trusted architectural construction firm, known for uncompromising structural longevity.

Our Mission

To deliver zero-delay, transparent turnkey construction utilizing sustainable regional materials & 3D BIM technology.

Pillars of Integrity

Our Core Values

Every foundation laid and blueprint drawn is governed by four uncompromising architectural and ethical principles.

PILLAR 01

Engineering Excellence & Precision

We execute multi-stage structural audits, soil-load analysis, and seismic compliance testing to ensure your home withstands generations.

• Grade-A Fe-550 TMT steel reinforcement standard.
• Ultrasonic concrete testing on every slab pour.
• ISO-certified waterproof membrane guarantees for basements and roofs.
PILLAR 02

Transparency & On-Time Guarantee

No hidden billing, no material substitutions. Clients receive real-time mobile ERP updates with fixed delivery penalty clauses.

• Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) locked before construction.
• Daily site CCTV live-streams available to overseas clients.
• Milestone-linked escrow payment schedules.
PILLAR 03

Sustainable & Climate-Responsive Design

Designing for Indian microclimates with rainwater harvesting, courtyard cross-ventilation, and locally sourced stone materials.

• Passive solar orientation reducing air-conditioning loads by up to 35%.
• In-situ fly-ash brick fabrication and Kota stone flooring.
• On-site STP (Sewage Treatment Plant) recycling for landscaped gardens.
PILLAR 04

Scientific Vaastu & Modern Living

Integrating ancient Vaastu Shastra spatial flow seamlessly with contemporary open-plan luxury villa aesthetics.

• Directional optimization for Brahmasthan (central courtyard lightwell).
• North-East water feature placements paired with modern infinity edge pools.
• Customized architectural floorplans balancing traditions with zero space wastage.
